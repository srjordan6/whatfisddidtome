01In short

It started with a question. On February 2, 2026 I asked my son's coaches whether anyone in his training group had been moved up to Gold. Coach Justin Bilgri wrote, "There have been no move-ups at this time." Coach Daiki Sato wrote, "To be clear nobody has moved up." I then learned that another swimmer from his group was already practicing with Gold.

On February 25 I filed a grievance. I wrote, "Both you and Coach Daiki explicitly informed me that [the other swimmer] was not being promoted. I have since discovered this was untrue." The next day I filed a report with the District's ethics hotline. When I asked what times a swimmer needed to reach Gold, Coach Bilgri sent the time ranges of the boys already in Gold, not a published standard. The program's own group requirements say Gold is by "Invitation by Head Age Group Coach ONLY."

On March 25 my son was moved to Gold, and I asked for written advancement criteria every family could see. On March 27 at 7:06 PM, Swim and Dive Coordinator Melissa Howard wrote: "If you continue to be dissatisfied, we can provide you with contact information for other clubs in the area that may be better suited to your needs."

I kept asking. In May the District wrote that a Board appeal "will be dismissed" and that the process "stops at Level II." On June 9 at 4:35 PM, three days after a meet in Arkansas, Head Club Swim Coach Matthew Franks opened a Parent Code of Conduct investigation into me and named himself the investigator. That evening I filed complaints with USA Swimming against him, Ms. Howard, Coach Sato, and Coach Bilgri, and at 6:32 PM I filed an appeal with the Texas Education Agency. On July 7, the day after the U.S. Center for SafeSport declined the matter, a Summary of Investigation issued under the names of those same four people barred me from every Iron Horse event through December 31, 2026, under threat of criminal trespass. I have never been told who reported me or what they said.

My son left Iron Horse because I could no longer attend. The program took no action against him.

03The program belongs to the District

In the District's July 15, 2026 answer to my state appeal, its outside counsel wrote that Iron Horse is not within the Board of Trustees' oversight authority. In a July 30 filing in the same agency, the same counsel wrote that the District owns and manages Iron Horse Aquatics.

The District's own budgets settle it. Every year from 2022–23 through 2026–27, the District's published line item budget carries Iron Horse in its General Fund, fund 164, organization code 912, labeled "Iron Horse (Swim Club)." Iron Horse coaching salaries are charged to "DISTRICT PAYROLL." The 2022–23 budget, the last year the District published revenue detail, shows a line titled "REVENUE/SWIM TEAM" budgeted at $670,000.

The District codes its high school swim teams as function 36, Cocurricular/Extracurricular. It codes Iron Horse as function 61, Community Services. On June 15, 2026 the Board adopted the 2026–27 budget at the fund and function level, including $2,453,059 for Community Services. Iron Horse is paid for from that appropriation.

07The meet in Arkansas

Saturday, June 6, 2026 · NWAA Memorial Classic, Bentonville, Arkansas

The District's decisions date it June 5. In the morning my son finished second in the 50 meter breaststroke preliminary and qualified to swim again in the afternoon, in lane 5. When he stepped up to his block, another swimmer was in his place. He went to an official, who did nothing. I went to the entrance of the pool deck and asked the volunteer at the gate if I could speak with an official. She went and spoke with them. No official ever came to speak with me. I never spoke with, argued with, or threatened any official.

The officials put my son in the next heat, in lane 1. He was upset and finished sixth. I did not know the race had happened. He came over to me afterward. I was at the deck entrance, about 5 to 10 feet inside it. I told him not to just accept it and to speak up for himself. Then I wrote in the team chat. These are the messages, in order.

Total screw up with my boy today. I want to speak to who is running the meet. The only fair thing to do is run heat 2 of the 50 meter breast again.

Me

Hi Stephen, I received your message and understand your frustration regarding [my son]'s 50m Breaststroke entry. The issue was an honest mistake. Despite this, the Meet Director and Head Official worked together to find an available lane for [my son] so that he could still compete. At a meet of this size and level, accommodations like this are not typically made, and they should be recognized as a courtesy rather than an expectation. While I understand your concern for your athlete, I need to address your conduct during the situation. In the future, if there is an issue, please come directly to me rather than confronting meet officials. The Meet Director and Head Official are volunteers who dedicate a significant amount of time to providing athletes with a quality competition experience. They deserve to be treated with respect, even when frustrations arise. Additionally, parents are not permitted on the pool deck or to approach officials regarding meet operations. This is a direct violation of the parent code of conduct and cannot happen again. Moving forward, any concerns should be brought directly to me, and I will work through the appropriate channels to address them. Thank you for your cooperation and understanding. Best regards.

Daiki Sato

Mr. Jordan, That's not how this works. He got to race and that is acceptable. Most of the time at meets of this level he wouldn't even get the opportunity to compete. Mistakes happen and this was not a mistake at the hands of the adults and they fixed it as best a they could. I understand the frustration but you also need to know that officials have the right to remove parents and spectators from the meet and if they do so because you are not abiding by the expectations, that is out of Coach Daiki's control. Thank you for understanding.

Melissa Howard

I asked for a meeting the next morning with Coach Sato and the meet director.

A meeting won't be necessary. And accommodations were made by finding another spot for him. [My son] getting to swim is a win and that the only time events are re-swum is if there is a malfunction with the timing system. Whole events are never just re-swum because one swimmer was in the wrong heat and lane.

Melissa Howard

I am going to let this drop this time, but coaches need to make certain that their swimmers are in the right event, heat and lane. You have to double check young kids.

Me

Yes, that is what the pre-race communication is for. [My son] and I talked before his race so he knew what heat and lane he needed to be. This was an accident and an isolated incident.

Daiki Sato

Coach Sato's message says I confronted meet officials. I asked a gate volunteer to bring one to me, and none came.

On June 11 I answered Coach Franks in writing and asked whether the office investigating me knew about my other pending matters. July 7 and 11, 2026

The night of July 7 I asked the Collin County district court for a temporary restraining order. I withdrew that case on July 11.

Texas Education Code Section 37.105

The statute says who may refuse a person entry to or eject a person from district property: an administrator, a school resource officer, or a district peace officer. It requires a verbal warning, a record of that warning, and written information about how to appeal. On September 17 I asked the District in writing for those records. I have not received them.

09The appeals

July 20, 2026, 6:00 PM · Deidra Rucker

Your allegations that Frisco ISD employees (Iron Horse Aquatics) retaliated against you, Mr. Jordan, for engaging in protected reporting/formal complaint filing is unsubstantiated.

Deidra Rucker, Associate Director of Athletics

Ms. Rucker had denied my criteria grievance at Level II in May. She applied Board Policy FFH (LOCAL), which defines retaliation as retaliation against a student involved in a discrimination or harassment complaint. She reasoned that the investigation "was initiated prior to Mr. Jordan filing a grievance alleging retaliation with Frisco ISD on June 15, 2026." She wrote that the report came from "staff affiliated with the Arkansas swimming venue." I had declined to meet with her because I had asked in writing that no one from Athletics review the grievance. When the portal returned an error, Complaint Resolution invited me on July 21 at 9:36 AM to send my appeal by email, and I did at 12:08 PM.

August 4, 2026, 4:01 PM · Esther Kolni to Dr. Fouche, Mr. McClendon, and Ms. Anderson

This is NOT the first time Mr. Jordan has attempted to dictate how the District investigation and grievance processes work and he has already received explanation from both me and Shelley regarding what merits recusal of a staff member and what does not.

Esther Kolni. Internal email, produced in the District's record.

August 5, 2026 · U'iLani Womble, then me

At 2:04 PM Ms. Womble wrote: "I have confirmed that I am the next step in your appeal process. Would you like to meet Monday, 8/10 at 1PM or Tuesday, 8/11 at 1PM?" At 2:08 PM I wrote to her and to Dr. Fouche:

You and the athletic department can just kiss my ass. I warned you Todd, now starts the public campaign.

Me

At 4:08 PM Dr. Fouche forwarded it to Ms. Kolni and Ms. Anderson: "Another one to loop in." At 4:40 PM Ms. Kolni replied: "Oh my." I should not have written that email.

August 12, 2026 · Level I decision, U'iLani Womble

The decision denied all relief except one records item. It refused to identify the people who reported me, citing FERPA. It also stated: "The Athletic office has been made aware of grievances filed against Iron Horse Aquatics." FERPA protects student education records. The people who reported me are adults. If their statements concern only me, they are not education records. If they concern my son, I have a right as his parent to see them.

August 15 to 26, 2026

On August 15 I filed my second petition with the Texas Education Agency, Docket 159-R10-07-2026. On August 18 I filed my Level II appeal by email because the portal offered no way to appeal. Chris Burtch was designated as the Level II hearing officer, the third reviewer in a row from Athletics. I objected in writing. The hearing was held August 26 at 1:00 PM at the Athletic Office, 7210 Stadium Lane. I asked who designated him and whether he had prior involvement. Neither question has been answered.

September 14, 2026 · Chris Burtch, Level II decision

The investigation, completed on July 7, 2026, found that you, Stephen Jordan, acted in a manner that was disruptive and deemed as argumentative toward meet volunteers and verbally threatening toward a meet official. The investigation and consequences were based solely on your actions at a swim meet in Arkansas. No evidence supported retaliation by Iron Horse swim coaches or administrators.

Chris Burtch, Managing Director of Athletics

the investigation established that, as of March 25, 2026, Iron Horse Aquatics had published its group requirements on its website, and those group requirements are listed in the Iron Horse Aquatics Handbook.

Chris Burtch

March 25 is the day I asked for the criteria. The group requirements still say Gold is by "Invitation by Head Age Group Coach ONLY" and refer to the 2026–2027 season. The decision also gives wrong dates for my filings. It says I appealed on August 20; I appealed on August 18. It says the Level I decision issued August 19; it issued August 12.

September 14 to October 5, 2026

I appealed to the Board of Trustees, Reference Z006179-091626. In May the District had said the Board would not hear my criteria grievance. It agreed the Board would hear this one. On October 5 Ms. Bradley wrote, "The District will preserve the records accordingly," and Complaint Resolution wrote, "Receipt confirmed. Your attachments will be added to the Level 3 Record."

October 19, 2026

The Board of Trustees considers my appeal. Under the Board's procedures it decides on the written record unless two trustees request an oral presentation. Its written decision is due by November 18, 2026.

10The State

Texas Education Agency

Two appeals are pending before the Commissioner of Education. Docket 120-R10-06-2026 concerns advancement criteria. Docket 159-R10-07-2026 concerns retaliation. On September 16 the District filed a 116 page local record in the retaliation docket. Every page carries the docket number of the other appeal, the record ends on August 5, and it does not include the Level II proceeding. I filed objections on September 18.

Texas Attorney General

I made three requests under the Public Information Act. In Open Records Letter Ruling OR2026-041431, issued September 18, the Attorney General accepted the District's representation that it reasonably anticipated litigation with me, and allowed most of the records to be withheld until that litigation ends. The second request was withheld on the same ground. A ruling on the third, which asks for program contracts, accounts, and salaries, is pending.

11What I got wrong

I went onto the pool deck in Bentonville, about 5 to 10 feet inside the entrance. I told my son not to accept what had happened to him. My first message to his coach began "Total screw up." On August 5 I told a District administrator and the Superintendent to kiss my ass and threatened a public campaign. Every one of those is quoted above, because a reader should be able to judge the rest of this page knowing them.

12What I am asking for

About the program: published, objective advancement criteria for Iron Horse Aquatics that apply to every family, applied consistently.

About what happened to me, in my appeal to the Board: that the decisions below be set aside, the July 7 suspension and its finding withdrawn, the witness statements and identities produced to me, and the matter reinvestigated by someone outside Athletics. I also asked the Board to find that Ms. Howard, Coach Franks, Coach Sato, and Coach Bilgri retaliated against me and to terminate their employment.

My son no longer swims for Iron Horse. Nothing I am asking for benefits him.
